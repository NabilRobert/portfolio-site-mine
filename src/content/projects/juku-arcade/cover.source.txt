cover.svg: an original illustration, drawn by hand as SVG for this site in the
style of the other covers ("JUKU" at the top, "ARCADE" letter-spaced at the
bottom), using only the site's palette. It uses no outside material.

It shows a juku-arcade board as the project's README describes it
(NabilRobert/juku-arcade, main at aeed90f): kanji word cards (本, 三日, 上)
above a fan of reading cards (ほん, みっか, うえ), with one decoy, みか, a
misreading of みっか that drops the small っ, as on the live game's N5 board. The caption "A Japanese
learning game" says what it is.

Lettering: Cinzel, and Noto Serif JP for the Japanese (both SIL Open Font
License 1.1), converted to outlines, as on the other covers. Drawn on
26 November 2026.

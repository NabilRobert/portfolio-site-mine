cover.svg: an original illustration, drawn by hand as SVG for this site in the
style of the other covers ("TRACE" at the top, "OBSERVABILITY" letter-spaced at
the bottom), using only the site's palette. It uses no outside material.

It shows a trace as a waterfall of spans, each nested under the step that called
it: the request and the agent's two tool calls (blues), the MCP server handling
the first (red) and its stages (rust, ink), and the second call stopping at a
refused step (the ring). It is a picture of the idea, not of a particular trace.

The caption "+0.39 ms per call" is the measured median overhead of tracing on an
MCP-backbone tool call, from docs/mcp-backbone-overhead.md in agent-observability
(branch fix/overhead-tails at d5fb5cc; not yet public).

Lettering re-set on 28 October 2026 for a simpler, book-cover look: the
wordmark, the caption and the bottom word are centred and set in Cinzel, the
other labels in Cinzel and the kanji in Noto Serif JP (both SIL Open Font
License 1.1), all converted to outlines. The illustration is unchanged.

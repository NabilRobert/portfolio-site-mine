cover.svg: an original illustration, drawn by hand as SVG for this site in the
style of the other covers ("GATE" at the top, "SECURITY" letter-spaced at the
bottom), using only the site's palette. It uses no outside material.

It shows an AI agent (left), tool servers (right) and the gateway between them,
with a keyhole (callers need a key). Tool outputs pass through the gateway;
one marked "!" (a prompt injection) is stopped at it. The chain along the ground
is the tamper-evident record, each entry linked to the one before.

The caption "Attacks that worked: 12 -> 5" is the published result on main in
https://github.com/NabilRobert/mcp-security-gateway: of 12 planted attacks, 12
took effect through the gateway with screening off, and 5 with it on.

Lettering re-set on 28 October 2026 for a simpler, book-cover look: the
wordmark, the caption and the bottom word are centred and set in Cinzel, the
other labels in Cinzel and the kanji in Noto Serif JP (both SIL Open Font
License 1.1), all converted to outlines. The illustration is unchanged.

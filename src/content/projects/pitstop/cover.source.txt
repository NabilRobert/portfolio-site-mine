cover.svg: an original illustration, drawn by hand as SVG for this site in the
style of the other two covers ("PIT" at the top, "STOP" letter-spaced at the
bottom, on the site's red), using only the site's palette.

It shows Pitstop's four agents as a pit crew around a car marked "AI", each
with an initial and a prop for their role: Anna (analysis) a tablet with a
chart, Rico (recommendations) a sign with an arrow, Sammy (summaries) a
clipboard, and Ciri (the critic) the chequered flag. The idea of a pit crew
servicing a car came from a stock illustration Arthur shared as a mood
reference; no figures, poses, car or layout were traced or copied from it.

The dashboard screenshot that the previous cover framed is now on the project
page: see dashboard-overview.source.txt.

cover.svg: an original illustration, drawn by hand as SVG for this site in the
style of the other covers ("BISECT" at the top, "DEBUGGING" letter-spaced at the
bottom), using only the site's palette. It uses no outside material.

It shows the planned method, not a result, as the project's measurement plan
describes it (NabilRobert/agent-bisect, docs/planning/measurement-plan.md, main
at ecd4924): a failing agent run of eight steps (model calls as circles, tool
calls as squares); above them, the failure rate after keeping each step of the
bad run, which jumps past the two thresholds (the dashed lines, 0.2 and 0.8) at
the decisive step, in yellow; below, the binary search narrowing to that step.
The bar heights are illustrative, not data: nothing has been measured yet. The
caption "Finding the broken step" says what it is.

Lettering: Cinzel (SIL Open Font License 1.1), converted to outlines, as on the
other covers. Drawn on 26 November 2026.
